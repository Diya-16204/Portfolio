A broad toolkit.

From a React interface to REST endpoints, data models and a usable workflow. I’m drawn to the connections between layers.

B

Make the decision legible.

In my ML work, I care about what a model takes in, how its output is used, and where human judgment still belongs.

Stay close to the evidence.
